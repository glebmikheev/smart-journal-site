01WHY IT EXISTS

Saving something isn’t the same as finding it again.

A useful idea rarely lives in one place. It is scattered across a note, a document, a conversation. Weeks later, you remember the connection — but not the words.

Smart Journal is built around that moment: bringing your material together, helping you find the meaning, and making the connections easier to inspect.

02A MORE USEFUL WORKSPACE

From saved material
to connected knowledge.

A calm interface. A few deliberate tools. Enough structure to stay useful.

01 /

Bring your sources together.

Notes, documents and media belong in one library. Keep the originals, organise with groups and tags, and add the properties that matter to you.

PDF · Markdown · Text · Media
02 /

Search the idea, not just the words.

Use a phrase you remember, even when the wording is different. Multilingual search brings back relevant passages with their original context.

Russian · English · French
03 /

Connect, with something to go on.

Explore suggests relationships and shows supporting passages. Inspect the evidence, accept what is useful, and keep the final decision yours.

Sources first. Your judgement last.
03DESIGNED WITH CARE

Quiet on the surface.
Considered underneath.

The interface and the local core are separate by design. Your sources stay distinct from AI suggestions, and background work stays visible and recoverable.

Python CoreReactLocal storage
01

Local-first, by choice.

Store your workspace on your computer. Choose local models for local processing, or explicitly configure a cloud provider.

02

Evidence has a place.

A suggested relationship stays a suggestion until reviewed. Supporting sources remain part of the connection.

03

Progress you can see.

Import in the background, pause the queue, watch model readiness, and back up or restore your workspace.
